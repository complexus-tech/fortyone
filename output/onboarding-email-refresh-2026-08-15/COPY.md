# Copy deck

## The campaign idea

**Most project tools start with a Task. FortyOne starts with the reason it deserves one.**

The sequence follows one idea from beginning to end:

    customer signal → decision → Objective → Task → owner → real week

That is the distinction every email should make visible. FortyOne is not another place to store work after the important thinking is over. It preserves more of the thread: why the work exists, what it is meant to change, who can own it, and whether the plan fits reality.

The voice uses compressed tension rather than startup hype:

- one sharp observation;
- one concrete consequence;
- one product mechanism;
- one action that completes the thought.

Avoid category language such as “AI teammate,” “work smarter,” “align and ship faster,” “all-in-one,” “seamless,” “unlock,” “supercharge,” and “focus on what matters.” Those phrases make FortyOne disappear into the category.

All greetings use Brevo's fallback filter so a missing name renders as “there”:
