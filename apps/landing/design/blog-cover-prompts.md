## productivity

Asset: `public/images/blogs/productivity-editorial.webp`

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium editorial cover for a FortyOne project-management blog. Generate ONE finished wide landscape image, 16:9, 1536x864 or similar. Visual inspiration: Calendly's new blog cover art, which collages natural candid professional photography with art-directed floating translucent software UI over soft atmospheric multicolor backgrounds. Clean considered composition, fine subtle grain, generous air, realistic warm daylight, sophisticated restrained glass with soft shadows. All corners of the inset photo and UI sheets should be smooth squircles. The image fills the rectangle edge-to-edge without an outer frame. No headline, no wordmark, no logos, no watermark, no browser chrome, no giant gradients or generic 3D clipart. Keep visual detail comfortably within 7% safe margins. UI text should be sparse, crisp, dark and readable. Topic: a productive team with all work connected. Sage green and soft cream foreground with a little muted blue and peach in the background. On the right a candid editorial portrait crop of a Black woman product lead with curly hair wearing a warm cream top, smiling subtly as she works on a laptop. On the left overlapping at most two exquisite white frosted UI sheets: a small task list titled "This week" with three checkable rows "Review feedback", "Plan next steps", "Ship the update", and a smaller green progress chip "On track". Tie the sheets together with one delicate curved connector. Human photo and UI equally important, composition feels like a beautifully art-directed magazine cover without headline.
