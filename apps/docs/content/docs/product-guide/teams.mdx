---
title: Teams
description: Give work a durable owner, workflow, and set of collaborators.
icon: Users
---

Teams group members and the work they own. Create teams around durable areas of
responsibility rather than every temporary initiative.

| Team setting      | Purpose                                                        |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name and code     | Identifies the team and prefixes its work items                |
| Members           | Defines the people who collaborate in the team                 |
| Privacy           | Controls who can discover and access team work                 |
| Workflow          | Defines the states used to move work from intake to completion |
| Labels            | Provides team-specific classification                          |
| GitHub automation | Maps repository activity to workflow changes                   |

Public teams can be discovered by workspace members. Private teams limit access
to members and administrators. A person’s workspace role and team membership
both matter; joining a workspace does not automatically grant access to every
private team.

Administrators manage teams under **Settings → Teams**. Members can join or
leave available public teams from the workspace navigation.
